How to use Buddy

Ask about anything on your screen. Buddy explains it out loud and draws on it while it talks. Use it to learn something new, get through an app, fix an error or check your own work.

⌃control+⌥option
Hold, ask, let go

Hold Control and Option, ask out loud, and let go. Your words show under the notch while you speak.

⌃control+⌥option+T 
Or type it

From any app. Return sends it, and it is answered the same way: out loud, drawn on the screen.

Basics

Asking

Ask the way you would ask a friend sitting next to you. Buddy sees the screen when you ask, so this, that line and here all work.

Out loud

Hold ⌃ ⌥, ask, let go. If it heard you wrong, the words under the notch show it before anything is sent. The answer starts within a few seconds, each sentence spoken with the marks that go with it.

Typed

Press ⌃ ⌥ T, or open the notch and click the chat box. ⇧ Return adds a line, Esc puts the box away and keeps what you typed. Up to 2,000 characters, enough for a pasted function or error.

Point at what you mean. Select some text before you ask, or rest the pointer on a line for a moment while you hold the keys, and that is what this means.

When idle, the notch disappears into the camera housing. When Buddy is busy, it widens: what Buddy is doing on the left, what it is about on the right.

Basics

Good questions

Ask about what is on screen, in your own words. Buddy fits the answer to the question: a sentence for a quick one, a drawn lesson for a big one.

why does this crash on the last elementRings the line and walks through the failing case
teach me how consistent hashing works properlyBuilds a lesson on a card, step by step
should I use Kafka or a queue hereGives the verdict first, then the reason
I didn't get thatExplains again from a completely different angle
what's a trieAnswers in a sentence or two
how do I export this as a PDFPoints at the first step, then guides you through the rest
Basics

Talking over it

While Buddy explains, say its name and speak, with no keys. It stops, and the notch says Buddy paused with your words. Without the name it carries on, so talking to someone in the room, or a video playing, never interrupts it. Asking a new question with the keys also stops it.

Buddy, wait, why is high the length?A new question. Buddy stops and answers it.
Buddy, hold onPauses mid-sentence.
go onCarries on from where it stopped. No name needed.
Buddy, slowerSlows the rest of the answer.
Buddy, go backSays the last sentence again.
Buddy, say that againPlays the whole answer again.

Replay in the open notch plays the last answer again, voice and drawing, with no new request and nothing spent. Buddy listens like this only while an answer plays, and the mic beside explaining in the notch shows it; ending the answer with Esc or Stop closes the microphone. With AirPods or another Bluetooth headset as the microphone, it listens through your Mac's own microphone instead, so the headset keeps sounding right; on a Mac with no microphone of its own it does not listen this way.

Basics

The open notch

Hover the notch to open it. Moving away closes it, and the app you were in stays in front.

Lesson

The last lesson as a paper card: its name, what it builds on, your question, the answer, and what to ask next. Replay, Open in Notebook and Copy sit beside it.

Chat

The whole day's conversation, spoken and typed, with Copy all. Each answer shows its time and which model answered.

Notebook and Map

Every lesson you have had, and the map of what you know. Each opens in its own window.

Settings

The gear. At most one alert shows above the lesson: a missing permission, a key problem, or a new version.

Learn

The board: learn something from scratch

Say teach me TCP from scratch or explain Raft on a whiteboard and the screen turns into paper. Buddy teaches the topic on it part by part, over as many questions as you like.

The notch says On the board the whole time, and a card lists the day's parts. Ask anything and it is answered on the board, adding to what is there. Esc stops a sentence, then leaves. done or Leave board also leave, and the whole board is kept in the Notebook as one page.

Learn

Teach it back

Explaining something is how you find out whether you understand it. Say let me explain consistent hashing back to you, check my understanding of binary search, or just teach it back after a lesson.

You talk, no keys

Take your time. A red meter shows Buddy is listening. Finish with I'm done, Return, or eight quiet seconds.

Your words, in purple

Buddy draws only what you said, the way you said it, wrong parts included. Nothing is added for you.

The gaps, in red

At most four, dashed so colour is never the only sign: a part left out, a link missing, or a ring with the correction beside it.

Then

Try again changes only the part you talk about. Fill the gaps has Buddy explain just those. Esc or Cancel throws it away.

What you got right counts as remembered and what you missed fades sooner on the map. The board is kept in the Notebook as one page.

Learn

Practice problems: hints before answers

On LeetCode, HackerRank, NeetCode and similar sites, Buddy helps in steps, so you still get to solve it. Say I'm stuck and it starts at the bottom.

A nudgeThe observation that leads to the idea.
The approachIdea, data structure and cost. No code.
By handThe first example, step by step.
The codeExplained, with a copy button.

Ask for another hint to go up a step, or show me the code to go straight to the top. A question about a concept on the page, like what's a heap, is answered normally without giving the problem away. Your step on each problem is remembered for two days.

Learn

Lectures

Watching a video lecture? Just ask, what did she mean by idempotent? There is nothing to switch on. Buddy knows what was said in the minute before.

The transcript first

From the page's own transcript or YouTube's captions, matched to the playback time.

The sound if needed

Where there is no transcript, and only after you allow System Audio Recording, that app's sound is turned into words on your Mac as it plays and filed by the video's time, in memory only, so pausing or rewinding still works. The lecturer on your speakers is never taken for you.

Never your calls

Not the microphone, not meetings or calls, not music apps. The notch shows a small screen glyph whenever sound is being kept.

Get it done

Getting through any app, step by step

Ask how to do something in the app in front of you, like where do I turn on dark mode. Buddy says one step and points at the exact button. Do it, and it notices and says the next one, until the task is done.

How do I export this?→ One step, pointing at the button→ You click. Buddy waits on your Mac→ The screen changed: the next step→ Done
Watching costs nothing

Between steps Buddy compares a tiny thumbnail of the screen on your Mac, and only asks again once the screen has changed. Going the wrong way? The next step says how to get back. Esc stops it.

Asked before? From memory.

A finished walkthrough is kept. Ask for the same task again in that app, in any words, and it replays with no model at all, only the voice. The notch shows from memory.

Fix

Errors on screen

When an error appears in a terminal, an editor or a judge's page, a soft red glow shows under the notch. Hover it and pick Explain this error, or tap ⌃ ⌥. Buddy rings the line it points to.

An error on screen.Terminal

Nothing is sent anywhere until you ask. Not now lets it go, and the same error is never offered twice.

Review

Reviewing your own work

Drawing a design in Excalidraw, draw.io, Miro, Freeform or any whiteboard app? Ask what am I missing? Buddy talks it through like a peer at the whiteboard: it marks your own boxes and sketches anything it would add as a dashed purple box beside where it belongs. It never scores the design.

Remember

The notebook

Every finished lesson becomes a page: its drawing, what was said, and links to teach it back or ask a follow-up. Open Notebook from the open notch.

Find anything

Lessons down the side, grouped by day. Search finds one by name, question or words.

Export a PDF

This page, this day, a range or everything, laid out like a printed notebook. It runs in the background and a notification opens it.

Yours to keep or clear

Kept on your Mac. More turns off keeping new lessons, or deletes a page, a day or every note, asking first.

Remember

Memory and the map

Buddy remembers the ideas it explains, across days. When a new question touches one, the answer builds on it, like the same trade-off as the consistent hashing from Tuesday, and the lesson shows Builds on with its name. If an explanation did not land, the next one uses a different example.

The map of what you know

Open Map from the notch. Every idea you have been taught, joined to the ideas it relates to, grouped by topic and coloured by how well you still remember it.

StrongFadingTime to review

Click an idea to see how it was last explained and the lessons it came from, then pick Quick recap or Teach it back. Memory stays on your Mac; Forget everything is in Settings.

Reference

What it costs

Buddy is free. It runs on your own API keys, so you pay the providers directly, and Spent today in Settings shows the running total.

1 to 6¢A typical question
About 1¢A walkthrough step, or a teach-back
Voice onlyA replayed how-to or answer replay costs nothing more than speech, or nothing at all
